This installs KISS Sorcar for you, the user who is logged in.

Everything goes into your home folder: the app in ~/.kiss, its commands in ~/.local/bin and its icon in the Applications folder of your home folder. You are not asked for an administrator password.

Your Mac must be online. The installer downloads Python, the libraries KISS Sorcar uses and a copy of the Chromium browser for its browser tools, about 1 GB in all, so the installation takes a few minutes.

Nothing else has to be on this Mac first: no Homebrew, no git and none of Apple's developer tools.
