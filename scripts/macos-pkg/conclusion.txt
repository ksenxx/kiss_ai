KISS Sorcar opens in its own window as soon as its installation is complete. On a slow connection the downloads can still be running now; the window then opens by itself when they are done, and ~/.kiss/install.log shows how far they are.

To open KISS Sorcar later, use the "KISS Sorcar" app in the Applications folder of your home folder.

Before your first task, open Settings in the app and enter the API key of a model provider.
